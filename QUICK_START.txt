Workflow:
1. Person applies for their own group.
2. Creator Admin approves application.
3. App creates separate group.
4. Creator Admin copies Group Admin link and sends it to group admin.
5. Group Admin opens own dashboard.
6. Group Admin shows Member QR.
7. Members scan QR and join that specific group.
